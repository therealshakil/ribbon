Like Spotify and Apple Music, downloading becomes one round icon beside Continue reading, not a second labelled button. Continue reading stays the only strong action. The size moves into Details, and the icon's name is spoken and shown on hover.

5. When there's no cover

A plain binding in the page's own greys, like a clothbound book with its title stamped on: title in Literata, a short rule, the author. No colour, so oxblood keeps its one meaning. At small sizes, where words can't be read, it shows the title's first letter (skipping “The”, “A” and “An”). The real cover replaces it as soon as one arrives.

Research behind this pass: web.dev's offline UX guidelines (keep content visible, label what needs a connection, don't rely on grey alone, give an offline library view); Kindle's All / Downloaded split and Apple Music's Downloaded section; Spotify's and Apple Music's single download icon that turns into progress, then a filled “downloaded” mark.
